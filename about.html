ABOUT

How I got here.

I started out running small businesses. The more I worked with the numbers behind them, the more I wanted to understand what they were telling me.

  1. 2018–19
    Co-founder · Pixel Gadgets

    It started with a shop.

    A physical store selling mobile phone gadgets, and my first look at the everyday work of running a business.

  2. 2019–22
    Co-founder · ZAS Wardrobe

    Marketing opened a new door.

    On a small team, marketing became my job. Learning to reach customers online introduced me to the analytics behind it.

  3. 2020–22
    Founder · LZ Brands

    The numbers became part of the work.

    I used digital marketing and SEO to grow our online presence, and started asking why some efforts worked better than others.

  4. 2023–24
    Volunteer Marketing Analyst & SEO · TECHSHIELD

    A new country, the same curiosity.

    After moving to the United States, I used Google Analytics and SEMrush to find website improvements, and my interest in analysis kept growing.

  5. 2025–now
    M.S. Business Analytics · University of Tulsa

    Giving that interest room to grow.

    Statistics, programming, forecasting, and applied projects are teaching me to test assumptions and judge how much to trust an answer.

  6. 2025–now
    Database Coordinator & Analyst · Albert Schweitzer Fellowship

    Putting it to work, every day.

    I build data systems, automate workflows, and develop reports, applying what I learn in class to real processes and people.

Different chapters, one growing question: what can the data tell me next?
